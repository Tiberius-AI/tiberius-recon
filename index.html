BEHIND THE SCENES Technical Architecture: How Recon Searches Portals, Property Records & Drafts Outreach
▾
1. MUNICIPAL PERMIT PORTAL SWEEPS (TIERS 2 & 3)

Recon queries municipal building portals (such as Accela Citizen Access, Tyler EnerGov, and regional permit feeds) for newly filed commercial tenant improvements, new shells, CapEx retrofits, and high-value residential permits matching your trade and minimum valuation.

2. COUNTY APPRAISAL DISTRICT (CAD) CROSS-REFERENCING

When property addresses are identified, Recon cross-references County Appraisal District parcel records to surface deeded ownership entities, assessed property values, building square footage, and lot acreage where available in public tax rolls.

3. SHOP KNOWLEDGE BASE ("SHOP DOSSIER") CALIBRATION

During onboarding, Recon scans your existing website (or uses the capabilities, licenses, bonding, and past project examples you enter) so every suggested introduction references your actual trade credentials rather than generic AI filler.

4. THREAD-LOCKED EMAIL & NATIVE SIM HANDOFF

Approved outreach can be dispatched via cloud email with thread-locked reply routing into your 3-Pane Conversation Room, or opened directly in your mobile phone's native SMS or Mail app (`sms:` / `mailto:`) so you stay in full control of every conversation.

STRAIGHT ANSWERS BEFORE YOU START

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How does Tiberius Recon find potential opportunities, and what sources are used? ▾

Depending on your plan tier and territory, Recon searches municipal building permit portals (such as Accela and EnerGov), County Appraisal District (CAD) property and deed records, and public commercial directories. It filters records by your target zip code, trade relevance, and minimum project valuation to organize morning briefings.

2. Are direct decision-maker contacts guaranteed on every single permit or record? ▾

Public permit and property filings vary by municipality and project type. When a permit lists a General Contractor, project applicant, or deeded owner entity, Recon cross-references available public business records and company directories to collect relevant contact information (such as a project manager, estimating desk, or office line) and always displays the underlying permit or parcel source reference so you can evaluate the lead before reaching out.

3. Does Recon send emails or texts automatically without my approval? ▾

No. You are always in control of your reputation and outreach. Recon prepares a suggested introduction for each card using your Shop Knowledge Base, and you can edit any word, skip any project, or approve the message when you are ready to send.

4. How does the 7-day free trial work, and how do I cancel if it's not a fit? ▾

When you enroll via Stripe checkout, $0.00 is charged today and your selected plan's full daily territory queue is unlocked immediately for 7 days. If you decide Recon isn't right for your shop, you can cancel anytime before Day 8 through your account portal or by replying to your welcome message and you will not be charged. After the 7-day trial, your subscription renews month-to-month at your chosen plan rate ($297, $597, or $897/month) with no long-term lock-in contracts.

5. What is the difference between B2B Deal Scout ($297), Commercial Permit Radar ($597), and Full Territory Dominion ($897)? ▾

B2B Deal Scout ($297/mo) focuses on B2B commercial prospecting (property managers, facility directors, and commercial property transitions) without municipal permit sweeps. Commercial & Sub Permit Radar ($597/mo) adds municipal commercial building permit intelligence (TI buildouts, new shells, CapEx retrofits) with a capacity limit of 2 contractors per trade per zip code. Full Territory Dominion ($897/mo) adds high-value residential permits, County Appraisal District (CAD) property record matching, and 100% single-contractor zip code exclusivity (1 of 1 slot) for your trade.

6. Do I need a new website to use Tiberius Recon? ▾

No. Tiberius Recon is a standalone opportunity research and outreach platform. During onboarding, Recon can scan your existing website (or use the details you enter manually) to build your Shop Knowledge Base. The Tiberius Smart Site ($250/month with $200/month ownership credit toward the $2,400 buyout) is completely optional.

7. Are the sample cards on this sales page live customer intelligence? ▾

The examples shown on this public sales page and in the public Demo Sandbox (such as Permit #BP-2026-08941 at 1420 Industrial Pkwy, Suite B) are labeled Example Recon Briefings / Illustrative Demo Records with simulated phone numbers so prospective contractors can test the approval and Conversation Room workflow safely without exposing active client intelligence.
