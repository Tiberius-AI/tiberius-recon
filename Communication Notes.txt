How We Decided to Handle Twilio SMS & A2PYes—in the prior Recon and Smart Sites architecture chats, we landed on a Master Twilio Account + Isolated Client Subaccounts model (Specifically to bypass GoHighLevel's LC Phone markups) paired with a Two-Stage "Bridge" Number Pool:Stage 1: The "Bridge" Pool (Day-1 Instant Delivery)
Because carrier A2P 10DLC Brand and Campaign vetting for a new contractor takes 5 to 14+ days, waiting on approval kills Day-1 momentum during a 7-Day Test Drive. To solve this, you keep a small pool of pre-verified numbers registered under Tiberius AI LLC's own approved Master A2P 10DLC campaign to handle immediate system alerts and Day-1 routing.   Stage 2: Dedicated Subaccount + Client A2P (Long-Term Production)
When a contractor converts to a paid plan (triggered via your Stripe webhook / hard-provisioning flow), your backend automatically spins up an isolated Twilio Subaccount (client_id) and submits their EIN and business details via Twilio's TrustHub / Secondary Customer Profile APIs. Once their A2P 10DLC campaign clears, their dedicated local number takes over seamlessly, keeping their reputation isolated from your master account.2. When the contractor sips their coffee and taps "Approve & Send" (or types a manual follow-up in the room), the UI sends the payload to your backend router, which dispatches by channel:   Email: Fires via Resend API (with a custom Reply-To address tied to that thread ID, e.g., reply+lead123@inbound.tiberius.ai) and logs the sent message into the room's timeline.SMS (if enabled): Fires via the Twilio Programmable Messaging API using that client's subaccount number and appends the outbound bubble to the timeline.Inbound Replies (The Universal Webhook Receiver)
To make replies show up back inside the same Conversation Room without the contractor checking three different apps, your backend listens on three webhook endpoints:/api/webhooks/resend (Inbound Email): Catches email replies from property owners or GCs, strips out the quoted email chain, matches the thread ID, and drops the reply directly into the Conversation Room chat view./api/webhooks/twilio (Inbound SMS): When a contact texts back, Twilio sends a POST payload (From, To, Body). Your server matches the phone number to the active lead_id, inserts the message into the database, updates the Conversation Room UI in real time, and pings the contractor./api/webhooks/retell (Inbound/Outbound Voice): Drops call logs, recordings, and transcripts straight into the same thread history.3.
